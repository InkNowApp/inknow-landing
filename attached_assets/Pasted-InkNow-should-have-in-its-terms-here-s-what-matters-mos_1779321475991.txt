InkNow should have in its terms — here's what matters most for a tattoo booking marketplace:

Payment & Disputes

Off-platform payments are not recognized (already discussed)
Chargeback/dispute policy — fraudulent chargebacks result in immediate account suspension pending review
InkNow's platform fee is non-refundable regardless of dispute outcome
Deposit refunds are governed by the artist's cancellation policy, not InkNow
No-Show & Cancellation

Clients who accumulate a certain number of no-shows may have their account restricted
Artists who repeatedly cancel booked appointments may lose platform access
What constitutes a "late cancellation" vs a standard one (within X hours of appointment)
Age Requirement

Users must be 18+ to use InkNow — tattooing minors is illegal in most jurisdictions and InkNow takes no responsibility if an artist performs work on an underage client
Artist Independent Contractor Status

Artists are independent contractors, not InkNow employees — InkNow is not liable for the quality, safety, or outcome of any tattoo or piercing service
Artists are solely responsible for maintaining proper licenses, health certifications, and complying with local regulations
Content & Portfolio

By submitting a consultation or completing a booking, clients grant InkNow and the artist permission to photograph and publish the completed tattoo in the artist's portfolio, unless opted out
Artists must own or have rights to all portfolio content they upload
Account Suspension Triggers

Explicitly list what gets an account locked: off-platform payments, chargebacks, no-shows, harassment, attempting to book through InkNow then pay outside — this removes all "I didn't know" defenses
Health & Safety Disclaimer

InkNow does not verify artist sterilization practices, health department certifications, or studio cleanliness — clients are responsible for vetting their artist's credentials before a session
Those are the ones that would actually hold up if something went wrong and you needed to point a user to a specific policy.
